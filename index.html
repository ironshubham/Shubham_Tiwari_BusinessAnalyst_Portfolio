Multichannel Order & Inventory Sync — Near Real-Time Integration

Near real-time sync accuracy · defect/failure rate under 2%

Context

This was an e-commerce marketplace integration project. The client was already selling on Amazon and wanted to expand onto TikTok Shop as a second marketplace — extending the same catalogue, order, and inventory operations across both channels without duplicating product setup effort or letting the two systems drift out of sync.

My Role

I owned the requirements for the multichannel sync solution end to end: defining the catalogue/SKU mapping rules across Amazon and TikTok Shop, specifying the order-and-inventory sync logic, and setting the validation criteria used to certify accuracy before go-live.

Approach

I mapped how catalogue and SKU data differed structurally between the two marketplaces — Amazon models product variations through a parent-child structure (one parent listing with child SKUs for attributes like size or colour), while TikTok Shop has no parent-child concept and treats every variation as its own standalone product. I defined a mapping standard that reconciled the two data models, then specified the order-and-inventory sync logic needed to move updates from batch to near real-time. I set defect and failure-rate thresholds up front and validated a test-store setup against them through SIT/UAT until the solution held consistently under a 2% failure rate.

Artifacts Produced

  • BRD
  • FRD
  • Catalogue/SKU mapping specification (parent-child vs. standalone)
  • Test-store setup wireframe

Outcome

Brought order and SKU processing to near real-time sync accuracy across Amazon and TikTok Shop, with defect and failure rates held under 2%.

ChurnGuard — AI Churn-Risk Detection Agent

Churn down ~30% · $120K+ in at-risk recurring revenue protected

Context

At Threecolts, a SaaS holding company for e-commerce seller tools, customer success had no systematic way to flag accounts at risk of churning before it was too late — intervention was reactive rather than predictive. The business needed a way to score accounts for churn risk before revenue was already lost.

My Role

I owned the requirements for ChurnGuard, an AI-powered churn-risk detection agent, end to end: running discovery with customer success and engineering, defining the scoring logic, and validating the model's inputs before a line of code was written.

Approach

I ran discovery workshops with customer success and engineering to identify which behavioural signals actually predicted churn, then defined a five-signal scoring model from that input. Before development began, I validated every risk signal by hand against CRM and product-usage data in SQL — treating the data as untrustworthy until proven otherwise, rather than handing engineering a spec and waiting to find out if it worked.

Artifacts Produced

  • Requirements & scoring-logic documentation
  • SQL validation queries

Outcome

Customer churn reduced by approximately 30%, protecting more than $120K in at-risk recurring revenue.
